## Opportunity Radar

**Problem:** Crypto account recovery after an email account has been compromised.

**Who has it:** Exchanges, custodial wallet services, users, and fraud/support teams.

**Current solution:** Email security, MFA, breach notifications, transaction alerts, manual reviews, and account freezes.

**Gap:** Signals are fragmented and reviewers must reconstruct an incident timeline quickly.

**Hypothesis:** An AI-assisted, human-controlled triage layer that assembles evidence, explains risk, and recommends bounded recovery actions. It must not hold funds, trade, or make final lockout decisions.

**Potential buyer:** Exchange, custodial wallet provider, or specialized security-support operation.

**Evidence:** One CNA report describes an increase in unauthorized access and says several affected email accounts appeared in earlier data breaches.

**Confidence:** Low to medium. Independent incident data, workflow interviews, competitive research, privacy feasibility, and false-positive testing are still required.
